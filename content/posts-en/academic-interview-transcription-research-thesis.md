## The 3 Types of Transcription in Academic Research

Depending on your academic discipline (Sociology, Education, Psychology, Linguistics, or Healthcare), dissertation committees may specify different transcription protocols:

### 1. Clean Verbatim (Standard Academic)
- Removes excessive verbal tics, stuttering, and involuntary filler words ("like", "you know", "um").
- Strictly preserves every argument, idea, and phrase spoken by the interviewee.
- The standard recommended format for theses in Business, Education, Healthcare, and Communications.

### 2. True Verbatim (Strict Literal)
- Captures every utterance: extended pauses, laughter, coughs, false starts, and emotional hesitations.
- Critical for Applied Linguistics, Speech Therapy, and Conversation Analysis.

### 3. Phonetic Transcription
- Uses the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) to record exact phonetic pronunciation. Reserved for specialized dialectology and phonetics research.
