3. **Professional Work Records:** Convert voice discussions with clients, partners, and colleagues into formal email summaries and project documentation.

## How to Transcribe WhatsApp Audio with Peguei o Texto

**Peguei o Texto** natively handles voice recordings generated by messaging platforms, including `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.m4a`, and `.mp3`.

### Step 1: Export the Audio File from WhatsApp
- **On iPhone:** Tap and hold the voice message > Tap **Forward** > Tap the **Share** icon in the bottom right corner > Tap **Save to Files**.
- **On Android:** Tap and hold the audio message > Tap the three dots in the top right corner > Tap **Share** > Save to device storage or your Google Drive.
- **On WhatsApp Web / Desktop:** Click the downward arrow on the voice message bubble > Click **Download**.

### Step 2: Upload to Peguei o Texto
Navigate to [Peguei o Texto WhatsApp Transcription](/en/transcribe-whatsapp). Select the **Upload Audio File** option and choose your downloaded audio message.

### Step 3: Get Instant Text & Summary
Within seconds, our AI engine transcribes the message with:
- Natural sentence punctuation and capital letters.
- High accuracy even with informal colloquial speech, regional slang, or faint background noise.
- An optional **AI Executive Summary** outlining the main takeaways, decisions, and requested actions.
