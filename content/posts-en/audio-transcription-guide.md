## What is audio transcription and how does it work?

**Audio transcription** is the process of converting spoken language from an audio or video recording into formatted, readable text.

Professional-grade transcription goes far beyond stringing words together. It requires:

1. **Phonetic Recognition**: Distinguishing homophones and discerning vocabulary from context.
2. **Punctuation & Intonation**: Detecting speech pauses, question intonations, and sentence boundaries to insert commas and periods accurately.
3. **Paragraph Segmentation**: Organizing spoken thoughts into cohesive blocks for effortless readability.
4. **Speaker Diarization**: Identifying and separating different speakers during panels, debates, and meetings.

### From manual typing to Deep Neural Networks
Until recently, speech recognition software required users to spend hours "training" their voice profile with scripted prompts.

Today, modern Deep Learning architectures (such as OpenAI Whisper and neural speech models) have changed the landscape. Trained on hundreds of thousands of hours of multilingual speech, these models comprehend regional accents, ambient background noise, and specialized industry jargon with up to **99% accuracy**.

## The 3 Main Types of Audio Transcription

1. **Verbatim (Word-for-Word)**: Captures every single utterance, including false starts, repetitions, and filler words ("um", "ah"). Essential for legal depositions and courtroom proceedings.
2. **Clean Verbatim (Standard / Intelligent)**: Removes verbal tics, stuttering, and fillers while keeping 100% of the original meaning and phrasing. Ideal for business meetings, podcasts, and video captions.
3. **Summarized / Executive Briefing**: Condenses long discussions into core themes, key decisions, and actionable checklists.
