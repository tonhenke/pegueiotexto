## 3 Tips for Maximum MP3 Transcription Accuracy

1. **Minimize Background Murmur:** If recording interviews in public venues, use a directional lapel or shotgun microphone to isolate the primary voice.
2. **Avoid Audio Clipping:** Ensure recording levels do not peak into the red distortion zone.
3. **Keep Original Sample Rates:** Avoid heavily compressing your MP3 below 128 kbps before transcription, as higher frequency detail helps neural networks distinguish soft consonant sounds.
