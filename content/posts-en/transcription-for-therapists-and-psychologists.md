## Understanding Ethical Dictation Workflows

In clinical therapy, ethical workflows typically take one of two paths:

1. **Full Session Recording (Requires Express Written Consent):** Used primarily in academic research, clinical supervision, or EMDR/training contexts where the patient signs an explicit Informed Consent document authorizing audio recording.
2. **Post-Session Clinician Dictation (Most Recommended & Prevalent):** The client leaves the room. The therapist pulls out their phone, opens the voice recorder, and speaks a brief 2-minute clinical summary using de-identified initials. The audio is then transcribed by [Peguei o Texto](/en).

## Privacy, HIPAA & Confidentiality Best Practices

Maintaining absolute confidentiality is non-negotiable in behavioral healthcare:

1. **De-Identification (Anonymization):** When dictating voice memos, never state full names, dates of birth, employers, or identifying addresses. Use client initials or case numbers (e.g., *"Client M.K., 34-year-old female..."*).
2. **Automatic File Purging:** At [Peguei o Texto](/en), audio files are encrypted during upload and automatically erased from temporary computing instances immediately following processing. Your dictations are never used to train public machine learning models.
